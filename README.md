# EchoEats User Manual
## Getting Started
* Open the project folder in a terminal.
* Type npm install and wait for the dependencies to download.
* Type npm start. The app opens automatically in your browser at localhost:3000.
* All your data is saved on your own device using the browser's local storage. No internet is needed once the app is running.
### Creating an Account
* On the Welcome page, tap Sign Up.
* Enter your email and a password (at least 8 characters with letters and numbers).
* Confirm the password.
* Choose your role: Consumer or Vendor.
* If you picked Vendor, enter your business name on the next screen.
* You are now logged in and taken to your home page.
### Logging In
* On the Welcome page, tap Log In.
* Enter your email and password.
* Tap Log In to enter the app.
### Forgetting Your Password
* On the Log In page, tap Forgot password?
* Enter the email associated with your account, then tap Continue.
* Set a new password (8 characters minimum, with letters and numbers).
* Re enter it to confirm, then tap Reset Password.
* Tap Back to Log In to use your new password.
### Editing Your Profile
* From the Profile page (consumer) or the gear icon in the navigation (vendor), tap Edit Profile.
* Change your email or business name if you want.
* To change your password, fill in the New password and Confirm new password fields.
* Enter your current password to verify your identity.
* Tap Save Changes.

## Using the App as a Consumer
### Setting Your Location
* At the top of the Browse page, tap the location chip showing your current city.
* Choose one of three options:
- Tap Use my current location to use your device's GPS.
- Tap any preset city from the list.
- Click anywhere on the small map to drop a pin manually.
* Tap Confirm Location.
### Browsing and Filtering Food
* Use the search bar at the top to type a food name.
* Pick a category from the Category dropdown if you want to narrow it down.
* Tap any number of dietary chips (Vegetarian, Vegan, Non Veg, Gluten Free, Halal, Kosher, Dairy Free, Nut Free, Keto) to filter to items that match all of them.
* Switch between List and Map view using the toggle.
* Tap any food card or map pin to open the full item details.
### Saving an Item to Favorites
* On any food card, tap the heart icon to save it.
* To see your saved items, tap the heart icon in the top navigation.
* The system will alert you when prices drop or when items are about to expire.
### Reserving a Food Item
* From the Browse page, tap the food item you want.
* Read the description and check the live price and timer.
* Tap Reserve Now.
* Choose a payment method:
- Cash to pay on pickup.
- Credit / Debit Card to enter card details.
- Apple Pay to authenticate with Face ID.
* If you picked card, enter the number, name, expiry, and CVV.
* Tap the Pay button.
* Wait for the authentication screen.
* You will see a confirmation showing your locked price and payment method.
### Viewing Notifications
* Tap the bell icon in the top navigation.
* Tap any notification to open the related food item.
* Use Mark all read or Clear all to manage them.
### Viewing Your Reservation History
* Tap the profile icon in the top navigation.
* Tap Reservation History.
* Browse the list of your past reservations, including date, payment method, and locked price.
* Tap any past reservation to revisit the original food item.

## Using the App as a Vendor
### Adding a New Food Listing
* From the Vendor Dashboard, tap + New Listing.
* Tap Upload photo to attach a JPG or PNG (up to 4 MB).
* Enter the Item Name, Description, Base Price, and Expiry in minutes.
* Pick a Category from the dropdown.
* Tap one or more Dietary tags that apply to the item.
* Set the Pickup Location:
- Tap Change.
- Use GPS, pick a city, or click on the map.
- Tap Done.
* Review the live preview at the top.
* Tap Add Item to save.
### Managing Your Listings
* From the Dashboard, tap View Listings.
* For any item you can:
- Tap Edit to update its details.
- Tap Mark Sold to manually mark it as reserved.
- Tap Delete to remove it permanently.
* Items that have passed their expiry will show a red EXPIRED tag and cannot be reserved by consumers anymore.
### Receiving Reservation Alerts
* Whenever a consumer reserves one of your items, the bell icon at the top will light up with a red badge.
* Tap the bell to open your notifications.
* Each notification shows who reserved the item, the price they paid, and the payment method they used.
* Tap Home in the top navigation to return to the Dashboard.
### Tracking Your Performance
* Open the Vendor Dashboard.
* Read your four metrics at the top: Items Listed, Items Sold, Revenue, Waste Reduced.
### Logging Out
* Tap Logout in the top right of the navigation bar to end your session.
